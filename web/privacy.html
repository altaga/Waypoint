Waypoint privacy

Waypoint stores only what you tell it to plan trips: traveler names, the country of each passport (not the passport number), trip dates, and expenses you log. Medication details are stored only if you give explicit consent, and are never sent to third parties.

Alexa passes an anonymous user id to Waypoint so your trips persist between sessions. Say "forget me" to delete everything Waypoint holds about you.

Waypoint is a simulated, self-hosted demo. It is not affiliated with Amazon. Rules change; verify with the official source before you travel.
